# M2 — cage-driven render skinning (game-exact cloth deformation)

_Successor brief to DELTAFRAMES.md. M1 is complete; what it found changes M2's plan — read
"What M1 settled" before planning. Prereq: FINDINGS.md F6/F7/F8, skill `d4browser-debugging`._

Goal unchanged: deform cloth RENDER verts from the simulated CAGE, as the game does, instead of
from cloth bones. Closes residual leg clipping and coarse folds.

---

## What M1 settled (do not re-derive — FINDINGS.md F6/F7/F8)

1. **`ptDeltaFrames` = `dmMtxMirror[]`**, one per cage vert, `rx@0 ry@16 rz@32 p@48`.
   Rows 0-2 are basis vectors; `p` is a **position** (zero on the barF cape, but a real field).
   Pure rotation, orthonormal to 3e-7, det +1, right-handed (`ry = rz x rx`).
   **`rx` = direction parent -> vertex** (residual 0.0016).
2. **The roll about `rx` is NOT derivable from cage data.** Six candidate families refuted with
   measured residuals (bind normals, triangle normals, tangent partner, world-axis up-reference,
   all four constraint classes, driver bind rotation in every composition). It reads as authored
   tangent space carried from the DCC export.
   **Consequence: do not reconstruct these frames — USE them.**
3. **There is no authored render->cage binding.** All 27 `ClothData` slots are accounted for by
   name via `definitions.json`. The binding must be **computed at load**.
4. **The `_sim` submesh IS the cage** — 77/77 verts coincident at **0.000 mm**, cage
   `triangleCount` 120 == the `_sim` submesh's 120 tris. It needs no binding and must not be
   rendered. What needs binding is the **visible** garment submesh (`barF_base03_TRS_mat`, and the
   fur), which is *not* coincident with the cage.
5. **Cage and render share one coordinate space** — byte-identical, z-up, no conversion or scale at
   rest. `zUpToYUp` is display-time only.
6. **`definitions.json` is the authoritative schema** (mounted at the d4data path; also
   github.com/DiabloTools/d4data). Look layouts up; do not reverse-engineer.
   **But verify component order against data** — declared `dmFrameMirror{q,p,s}` is serialized
   **position-first** (`p,q,s`), confirmed on three independent structs.

## M2.1 — binding (offline first, in the harness)

Compute, for each visible cloth render vert, a binding to the cage. Do this in `tools/d4cloth`
and validate it BEFORE touching the app.

1. Identify the visible cloth submeshes. `_sim` submeshes are the cage (exclude them); the garment
   submeshes are the target. The harness currently parses ClothData only — it needs the
   vertex-buffer / segment / sub-object scan (mirror `ModelParser::scanVertexBuffers` +
   `scanSegments` + `findSubObjects`; VB stride 36 simple / 44 skinned, positions at offset 0).
2. Per render vert: nearest cage **triangle**, barycentric coords within it, and a signed offset
   along the triangle normal. Store `(tri, u, v, w, offset)`. No per-model constants; no magic
   radii — if a vert has no cage triangle within a data-derived bound, it stays bone-skinned.
3. **Validation gate:** reconstruct every bound render vert at REST from its binding and compare
   to its authored position. Target **< 1 mm**; report the residual distribution, not a pass/fail.
   Add as F9 with measured numbers, and goldens for 2 pieces.

_Note: the corresponding M1.2 gate was degenerate (the `_sim` verts ARE the cage, so it was exact
by construction). This one is the real test._

## M2.2 — runtime deformation

After the cage solve, per bound render vert: interpolate position from the triangle's simulated
particles by barycentric weight, and displace along the **simulated** triangle normal by the stored
offset. Use `ptDeltaFrames` (posed by the sim) to transport the vert's **normal and tangent** —
that is what the frames are for, and why the roll being authored is fine.

- Keep the bone path for every non-cloth vert and as the fallback for any unbound cloth vert.
- Keep the bone drive for the overlay and the exporter (the exporter ships the bind pose).
- Gate the whole path behind a setting, default OFF, until verified against in-game footage.

## Two open leads found in M1 (deliberately NOT actioned — decide before starting)

**Capsules carry authored fields the app ignores.** `dmClothCapsuleDefMirror` has `scale`
(vec4, all `1,1,1,1` on the barF cape), a **`hide`** flag (all 0), `radius1 == radius2` (no taper),
`friction` 0.10 and `solver` 2. The app substitutes a hardcoded **0.52** radius multiplier for all
of it. A global constant standing in for authored data violates the "no per-model constants"
guardrail and is the leading suspect for residual clipping — but 0.52 is visually verified and a
blind revert regressed before. If M2 changes collision behaviour, re-test 0.52 rather than
assuming it still holds.

**Planes are one unknown away from usable.** `dmClothPlaneDefMirror = localTransform(32) |
stiffness@32 | friction@36 | boneIndex@40`. Every plane in the corpus is uniform: **identity
rotation**, position `(0,0,d,0)` with d = 0.034-0.070, stiffness 1.000, friction 0.100, bone 0.
Only the normal's axis convention (and what bone 0 is in that space) remains. Planes stay **OFF**
for M2 regardless — do not bundle this in.
