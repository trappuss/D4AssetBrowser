Contract & data
RigMathnewOne matrix convention (row-vector v·M) for the whole family — settled decision; the three tools disagreed and the exporter bugs came from that.
IGameStore + BackgroundIndexnewThe index shape was hand-rolled eleven times (7 D4 singletons, FOX's three indexes, POE2's loader) and the copies disagreed; one contract, thread-safe load set, worker-thread loading for every game.
Lazy clips (Clip::lazy / bakeClip)FOXFOX decoded one clip at a time; baking all of a Fox model's 20,921 clips at load measured 99 s.
